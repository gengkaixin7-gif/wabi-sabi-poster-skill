# English Minimal: Quiet Human–Product Interaction

## Intent

Create a sparse editorial poster with one restrained English headline, a clearly identifiable product, and a believable human gesture. The mood is tactile, slow, natural, and slightly vintage—not glossy fashion spectacle or futuristic advertising.

## Content boundary

- Show one headline and one small identity group containing the brand and full product name. Model or capacity may be included when supplied.
- Omit feature lists, price, campaign dates, coupon conditions, and calls to action by default.
- If the user requires complete promotion details, route to the detailed English branch instead of squeezing them into this layout.
- Use only verified claims and exact brand spelling.

## Interaction

- Prefer a quiet action: holding, pouring, adjusting, carrying, resting beside, opening, or preparing to use the product.
- Hands and posture must respect the product's real scale, weight, grip, and operating logic.
- A symbolic oversized product is allowed only when it strengthens the concept without distorting structure; use a calm pose and grounded shadow rather than a theatrical stunt.
- Wardrobe should be plain, textured, and low saturation. Linen, cotton, knit, worn leather, and matte workwear are suitable when relevant; do not force an ethnic or Japanese costume.

## Layering and type

1. Natural or studio-built tactile background with visible but quiet surface variation.
2. One large headline, optionally with slight letterpress wear or faded ink. Keep spelling and letterforms intact.
3. Person and product in front, with accurate edges, shared light, and contact shadows.
4. One compact identity group in safe negative space. The full product name must remain readable.

Use a warm serif, humanist sans serif, or a restrained pairing of the two. Do not stretch type, imitate calligraphy, or cover faces, hands, logos, and key product structures.

## Check

- The result still reads as minimal when viewed from a distance.
- Interaction is quiet and physically credible.
- Product color, structure, and logo match the reference.
- Texture feels material rather than like a heavy filter.
- No detailed sales panel, card wall, neon effect, or decorative cultural cliché has entered the composition.
